Lauren Shoemaker Porfolio

Bridging information literacy & Scientific Inquiry

Library Media Specialist & Educator dedicated to fostering critical thinking, digital citazenship, and a love for discovery

This portfolio bridges the gap between my love for science and research. I have taught science for 5 years, showcasing a love for research in the way students generate their own data and analyze it with a fine toothed comb. In this case, I am simply adding onto my area of expertise. I love teaching research and I have such a love for reading that it seemed like an obvious choice to add being a librarian to my career.

What will you find?

The purpose of this portfolio is to showcase what I have learned in my graduate studies to me a library media specialist. You will find a showcase of books for the upcoming holidays, why community is important in the library, how research and ethics are important and the backbone of any library, and how the library can be a cross curricular research hub in order to allow for the entire school to have access to high quality resources.
