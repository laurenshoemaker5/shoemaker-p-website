Research and Ethics

Being able to be truthful in our research helps us be able to learn and grow as a society. This section will outline different research strategies and citation tools to help students and teachers be able to create supports for the classroom. This will help students improve digital citizenship and be good stewards of information.

Ethical Considerations Applications in the Library Impact on Student Learning
Intellectual Freedom Protecting the user's right to seek information without censorship. Ensures a diverse range of viewpoints in the research process.
User Privacy Safeguarding "circulation" and "search" data from third-party tracking. Safeguarding "circulation" and "search" data from third-party tracking.
Information Literacy Teaching Users to evaluate the credibily and bias of sources Empowers students to look beyond surface level searching

Boolean Operators

How do to transform your google searching into full fledged research?

  • Boolean Operator

      AND
      OR
      NOT
      " "

    1. Function

      Narrows search; both terms must appear in your search
      Broadens search; either term can appear
      Excludes a term from your search
      Searches for an exact phrase

    2. Example

      Teenagers AND Social Media
      Teenagers OR Juveniles
      Social Media NOT Instagram
      "Learning Strategies for Teens and Young Adults"
